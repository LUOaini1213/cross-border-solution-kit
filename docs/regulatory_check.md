# Regulatory check log

Regulatory mistakes are the main risk in this project, so every regulatory or tax statement the kit relies on is kept in `data/reference/regulatory_statements.yaml` with its sources and status, and was checked twice:

1. **Research pass (2026-11-15).** Each statement was written from the official page or legal text (government, statutory body or treaty secretariat), saved locally and read in full; secondary sources were only used to find the official one.
2. **Separate re-check pass.** `scripts/check_regulatory_sources.py` fetched every cited source again and searched for the evidence phrase recorded with the statement; sources that cannot be read by a script (an image-only gazette page, blocked sites, secondary-only evidence) were re-read by hand and are marked `manual`. The result per statement is below.

## Corrections to the project brief

- **ATA Carnet issuer in Singapore.** The brief named the Singapore Business Federation. Singapore Customs (page updated 31 Jul 2026), the ICC directory of national guaranteeing associations and SICC itself name the **Singapore International Chamber of Commerce (SICC)**; no carnet role for SBF was found (statement `SG-ATA-ISSUER`).
- **ATA purposes accepted by Singapore.** The brief listed commercial samples among ATA uses. For imports into Singapore, Singapore Customs lists only exhibitions/fairs, professional equipment and scientific equipment for tests or demonstrations (`SG-ATA-SCOPE`, `SG-ATA-NO-SAMPLES`).
- **Thailand's low-value rule.** The THB 1,500 duty exemption no longer applies: Customs Notification 219/2568 limits the exemption to items worth up to 1 baht from 1 January 2026 (`TH-DUTY-1BAHT`).

## Not verified (placeholders or caveats in the kit)

- `ID-ABOVE-1500`: Consignments declared on a PIBK pay MFN duty and import taxes under the general rules (PMK 96/2023 Art. 29(5) as amended). That consignments above USD 1,500 must use a PIBK, and the MFN rates for the template products, were not verified.
- `ID-IMPORT-VALUE`: The kit takes Indonesia's VAT import value as customs value (CIF) plus import duty. The statutory definition was not retrieved, so this base is an assumption.
- `ID-LICENSING`: Bulk (non-consignment) imports of textiles, footwear, bags and electronics may need trade-ministry import approvals or technical requirements; these were not verified.
